About us

Knowledge, preparation and honest advice.

Carology Customs was created to save customers time and money without lowering standards. Every vehicle is assessed carefully, prepared properly and road tested before it is offered for sale.

As a small family business, we know our stock inside out and can give customers the sort of direct, informed advice that is often lost in larger dealerships.

  • Prestige dealership experience
  • Specialist diagnostic capability
  • Carefully prepared used vehicles
  • Flexible appointment-based service
